On March 31, 2026 (financial year-end), what adjusting journal entry should be passed to record the **prepaid insurance** relating to the next financial year?

- **A)** Insurance A/c Dr. ₹11,000 / To Prepaid Insurance A/c ₹11,000
- **B)** Prepaid Insurance A/c Dr. ₹1,000 / To Insurance A/c ₹1,000
- **C)** Prepaid Insurance A/c Dr. ₹12,000 / To Cash A/c ₹12,000
- **D)** Prepaid Insurance A/c Dr. ₹11,000 / To Insurance A/c ₹11,000

### Question 10 *(Challenging)*
A First-Year B.Com student recorded *"Paid ₹9,000 from business cash towards the proprietor's daughter's college fees"* as `Education Expense A/c Dr. ₹9,000 / To Cash A/c ₹9,000`. Why is this entry incorrect, and what is the correct journal entry?

- **A)** Cash A/c should be debited instead of credited; correct entry: `Cash A/c Dr. ₹9,000 / To Education Expense A/c ₹9,000`.
- **B)** Under the Business Entity Concept, personal expenses of the owner are not business expenses; correct entry: `Drawings A/c Dr. ₹9,000 / To Cash A/c ₹9,000`.
- **C)** Capital A/c must be credited instead of Cash A/c; correct entry: `Education Expense A/c Dr. ₹9,000 / To Capital A/c ₹9,000`.
- **D)** The entry is correct because the cash was paid directly out of the business cash drawer.
