## 5. Accounting Conventions & Items Flagged for Faculty Review

Rather than making silent assumptions where Indian university syllabi or textbooks show minor academic variations, the following **five conventions** are explicitly documented for review and approval before application development begins:

1. **Classification of `Purchases A/c` and `Sales A/c` (Nominal Account vs. Real Account):**
   - *Choice Made:* Classified consistently as **Nominal Accounts** (Expense and Revenue), aligned with ICAI CA Foundation and modern B.Com curricula (since they close to the Trading Account).
   - *Variation to Review:* Some traditional Indian university textbooks classify `Purchases A/c` and `Sales A/c` as **Real Accounts** (subdivisions of *Goods A/c*), which makes crediting `Purchases A/c` in Exercise 5B (*goods withdrawn for personal use*) explainable via *"Credit what goes out"* rather than *"reduction of a Nominal expense"*.
   - *Action Requested:* Please confirm whether **Nominal Account** (current implementation) should be retained or switched to **Real Account** based on your target university syllabus.

2. **Subtype Classification of `Capital A/c` and `Drawings A/c` (Representative vs. Natural Personal Account):**
   - *Choice Made:* Classified consistently as **Representative Personal Accounts** (representing the proprietor's equity and withdrawals).
   - *Variation to Review:* Because a sole proprietor is a natural person, some Indian B.Com textbooks classify `Capital A/c` and `Drawings A/c` directly under **Natural Personal Accounts**.
   - *Action Requested:* Please confirm if **Representative Personal Account** aligns with your preferred curriculum standard.

3. **Depreciation Recording Method in Exercise 5A (Direct Reduction vs. Provision for Depreciation):**
   - *Choice Made:* Used the direct asset-reduction entry (`Depreciation A/c Dr. To Furniture A/c`), which is standard for First-Year B.Com Chapter 1 (Journal Entries).
   - *Variation to Review:* The `Provision for Depreciation A/c` (Accumulated Depreciation) method is typically introduced later in the dedicated *Depreciation Accounting* chapter.
   - *Action Requested:* Confirm that the direct reduction method is preferred for this introductory chapter.

4. **Exclusion of GST (Goods and Services Tax):**
   - *Choice Made:* All transactions are explicitly marked as **exclusive of GST** so beginner students master foundational double-entry rules before adding CGST/SGST/IGST computations.
   - *Action Requested:* Confirm whether GST journal entries should remain out of scope for Chapter 1 and be planned as a separate follow-up module (*Chapter 1B: Journal Entries with GST*).

5. **Same-Day Cheque Deposit Assumption (Worked Example 5):**
   - *Choice Made:* Worked Example 5 explicitly states *"deposited into the bank on the same day"* so `Bank A/c` is debited directly without ambiguity.
   - *Variation to Review:* If a cheque is received and deposited on a *later* date, current accounting practice uses `Cheques-in-Hand A/c`.
   - *Action Requested:* Confirm whether `Cheques-in-Hand A/c` should be deferred to the *Cash Book & Banking Transactions* chapter.
